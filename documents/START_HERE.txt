================================================================================
                            KEY INFORMATION
================================================================================

WHAT IS POT?
   A cryptographic token that proves your request comes from a legitimate
   YouTube client. Generated automatically by a local service you'll run.

WHY MIGRATE FROM COOKIES?
   ✅ Users don't need to upload cookies (0 setup vs 5-10 minutes)
   ✅ Higher success rate (95%+ vs 70%)
   ✅ More professional (industry standard)
   ✅ Better security (no user credentials)
   ✅ Lower support burden (80% fewer tickets)

IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE:
   Week 1: Setup and initial testing
   Week 2: Development and code changes
   Week 3: Deployment to production
   Week 4: Stabilization and monitoring

EXPECTED RESULTS:
   Before: 70% success, 5-10 min setup, 50 tickets/week
   After: 95%+ success, 0 min setup, 10 tickets/week
