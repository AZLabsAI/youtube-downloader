# 🍪 Cookie Upload - Quick Reference

## When You Need Cookies

### You'll See This Error:
```
YouTube detected automated access. 
Please upload your YouTube cookies to continue.
```

## How to Fix (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Install Cookie Extension
**Chrome/Edge:**
- Search "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" in Chrome Web Store
- Click "Add to Chrome/Edge"

**Firefox:**
- Search "cookies.txt" in Firefox Add-ons
- Click "Add to Firefox"

### Step 2: Export Your Cookies
1. Go to https://youtube.com
2. Make sure you're logged in
3. Click the cookie extension icon
4. Click "Export" or "Download"
5. Save the `cookies.txt` file

### Step 3: Upload to App
1. Open the YouTube Downloader app
2. Click the "🍪 YouTube Cookies" section
3. Click "📁 Upload cookies.txt"
4. Select your downloaded file
5. See "✓ Loaded" confirmation
6. Download your video!

## Important Notes

✅ **Safe & Secure**
- Cookies are used only for YOUR download
- Deleted immediately after (within seconds)
- Never stored permanently

✅ **Privacy Protected**
- Only YouTube/Google cookies are used
- Other cookies are ignored
- No logging of cookie data

✅ **Works Locally & Online**
- Same process for both
- Export from YouTube once
- Use for multiple downloads

## Troubleshooting

### "No valid cookies found"
→ Make sure you exported in "Netscape" format

### "No YouTube cookies found"  
→ Visit YouTube before exporting
→ Make sure you're logged in

### Still getting bot detection
→ Cookies may be expired
→ Export fresh cookies from YouTube

### Can't find the extension
→ Chrome: [Get cookies.txt LOCALLY](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/get-cookiestxt-locally/cclelndahbckbenkjhflpdbgdldlbecc)
→ Firefox: [cookies.txt](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/cookies-txt/)

## Extension Names

✅ **Recommended (Safe & Local)**
- "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" (Chrome/Edge)
- "cookies.txt" (Firefox)

❌ **Avoid**
- Extensions that send data to servers
- Extensions without "LOCALLY" in name
- Unknown or unverified extensions

## Cookie Lifetime

📅 **When to Re-upload:**
- If you get bot detection again
- After logging out of YouTube
- Every few weeks (cookies expire)

🔄 **Quick Re-upload:**
1. Click "Clear" button
2. Export fresh cookies
3. Upload again

## Support

📧 **Still Having Issues?**
- Check you're on YouTube before exporting
- Try a different browser
- Export fresh cookies
- Clear old cookies and re-upload

🎯 **Works Best When:**
- You're logged into YouTube
- Using fresh cookies (< 1 week old)
- Using recommended extensions
- Following exact steps above

**Remember:** Cookies are completely optional! Only needed if YouTube blocks the download.
